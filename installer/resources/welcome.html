Tatakan

A drag-and-drop shelf for files and a small panel that grows out of the notch.

This installs Tatakan.app into /Applications. It works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

After installing, Tatakan lives in the menu bar. There's no Dock icon. macOS will ask for Calendar, Bluetooth and Camera access the first time you use those features. You can say no and the rest still works. Nothing leaves your Mac.
